# Pre-Game Plan: Gnar / Ekko / Naafiri / Ashe / Seraphine
### vs Ambessa / Mundo / Cass / Senna / Nami

---

## Draft Overview
- Our comp archetype: Burst-heavy engage with scaling carries.
- Enemy comp archetype: Sustained damage, scaling, and disengage.
- **The one thing that decides this game: Our ability to burst down enemy carries before their sustained damage and tankiness overwhelm us.**

## Lane-by-Lane
- Top: Gnar wins 1v1, expected push/prio towards enemy. Ambessa is gank vulnerable if immobile.
- Mid: Cassiopeia wins 1v1 sustained, Naafiri wins short trades. Cassiopeia will push. Cassiopeia is gank vulnerable (no mobility).
- Bot: Senna/Nami win 1v1 poke. Expected push/prio towards our tower early. Both Ashe/Seraphine and Senna/Nami are gank vulnerable due to immobility.

## Gank Targets
- Best targets ranked: Cassiopeia (no mobility), Senna (no mobility), Nami (squishy, CC).
- Who NOT to gank into and why: Mundo (too tanky, high sustain, hard to kill, especially post-6).
- Which ally has the CC to set up your ganks: Gnar (Mega R/W), Naafiri (W), Ashe (R, W slow), Seraphine (E, R).

## Early Game Plan
- Suggested first-clear direction and why: Full clear towards bot side (Red -> Krugs -> Raptors -> Wolves -> Blue -> Gromp) to hit level 4 quickly and look for a gank on bot or mid, or invade Mundo. Mundo is slow and vulnerable early.
- Grubs vs dragon priority for THIS draft: Prioritize Grubs if top/mid priority allows, as it helps snowball our burst/dive lanes (Naafiri, Ekko) by taking plates. If Dragon is free, take it, but do not solo-force early objectives on spawn (per HR2). Dragon is usually worth at least a full grub spawn (per HR1).
- Invade/track risk: Mundo is very vulnerable to invades levels 1-4 due to slow clear and lack of escape/hard CC. Track him aggressively and look for steals or kills.

## Mid Game Plan
- Which side of the map to play, what to trade when objectives spawn: Play around mid and bot to enable Naafiri roams and Ashe/Seraphine pressure. Use Herald for mid tower. If enemy contests Dragon, look to trade for Herald or top tower.
- Whose power spikes to play around (ours and theirs): Play around Ekko level 6 and first item (Lich Bane/Rocketbelt), Naafiri first item (Dirk/Eclipse), and Ashe R for picks. Watch out for Mundo's tank item completion (Spirit Visage/Thornmail) and Cassiopeia's Lost Chapter/Mythic spike.

## Teamfight Plan
- Peel or dive? Primary target? Who must be protected on our side?: **Dive the enemy backline (Cassiopeia, Senna) with Ekko/Naafiri.** Peel for Ashe with Gnar/Seraphine if needed.
- Our engage tools vs theirs; who wins a 5v5 front-to-back: We have strong engage (Gnar R, Ashe R, Naafiri W, Ekko E/W, Seraphine R) for picks and burst. They have disengage (Nami R, Cassiopeia R) and a tanky frontline. We win if we can isolate and burst their carries; they win if they can survive our initial burst and kite.

## Watch-outs
- Mundo becoming unkillable: Avoid extended fights with Mundo once he gets tank items.
- Cassiopeia R: Be mindful of Cassiopeia's ult when diving, especially with Ekko/Naafiri.
- Nami R disengage: Nami's ult can disrupt our engages and create space for their carries.
- Senna scaling: Senna can become a significant threat if allowed to farm souls and scale.
