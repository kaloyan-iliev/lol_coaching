# Pre-Game Plan: Shen / Diana / Sylas / Aphelios / Janna
### vs Riven / Ekko / Locke / Ashe / Seraphine

---

## Draft Overview
- Our comp archetype: Engage/Peel with Scaling Hypercarry.
- Enemy comp archetype: Poke/Pick with Scaling AP Burst.
- **The one thing that decides this game: Our ability to survive enemy poke/picks and execute coordinated teamfights to protect Aphelios while diving their backline.**

## Lane-by-Lane
- Top: Riven wins 1v1 vs Shen, Riven pushes for prio, Riven is gankable but mobile.
- Mid: Sylas wins 1v1 vs Lux, Sylas pushes for prio, Lux is very gankable (immobile, squishy).
- Bot: Aphelios/Janna survive vs Ashe/Seraphine, enemy will poke for push, Ashe is immobile and gankable.

## Gank Targets
- Best targets ranked:
    1.  Lux (Mid): Immobile, squishy, Sylas has CC.
    2.  Ashe (Bot): Immobile, Janna has peel/slow.
    3.  Riven (Top): Mobile but can be locked down by Shen's taunt.
- Who NOT to gank into and why: Ekko - very slippery with E and R, wastes time pre-6.
- Which ally has the CC to set up your ganks: Sylas (E, W, Q), Shen (E), Janna (Q, W, R).

## Early Game Plan
- Suggested first-clear direction and why: Full clear bot-to-top. This paths towards Sylas (easy gank target) and Shen (needs help vs Riven). If Ekko starts top, this also allows a potential counter-gank mid/top.
- Grubs vs dragon priority for THIS draft: Prioritize Grubs if we have top/mid priority and Ekko is bot. Prioritize Dragon if we have bot/mid priority and Ekko is top. Do not solo force (per HR2). Dragon is valuable (per HR1).
- Invade/track risk: Ekko wants to farm to 6. He might try to invade our bot side if he sees us top. Track his start via laner leashes. Assume he starts Red for faster clear.

## Mid Game Plan
- Which side of the map to play, what to trade when objectives spawn: Play around mid/bot to scale Aphelios and secure Dragons. Use Sylas's mid priority to "ping pong" (COACHING KNOWLEDGE) to side lanes. If enemy takes Dragon, trade for Herald or top tower.
- Whose power spikes to play around:
    - Ours: Diana/Sylas Level 6, Aphelios 2-3 items.
    - Theirs: Ekko/Riven Level 6, Lux/Seraphine 2 items (poke damage).

## Teamfight Plan
- Peel or dive? Primary target? Who must be protected on our side?
    - Dive: Primary target is Lux/Ashe/Seraphine. Diana and Sylas can dive.
    - Peel: Protect Aphelios. Shen and Janna are key for peel.
- Our engage tools vs theirs; who wins a 5v5 front-to-back: Our engage (Shen E, Diana R, Sylas E) is strong. Their engage (Riven E-Q, Ekko E, Ashe R, Seraphine R) is also good. We win 5v5 front-to-back if we can peel for Aphelios and prevent Ekko/Riven from diving our backline. Our disengage (Janna R, Shen R) is strong.

## Watch-outs
- Ekko Level 6: His ganks and escapes become much stronger. Track his ult.
- Ashe/Lux/Seraphine ults: Global/long-range CC can set up picks or turn fights. Respect their range.
- Riven snowball: If Riven gets ahead, she can be unkillable. Keep her down.
- Objective flips: Enemy has strong objective steal potential (Ekko E-Q-Smite, Lux R). Secure objectives carefully.
