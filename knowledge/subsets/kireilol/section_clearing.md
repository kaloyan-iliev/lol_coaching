##Clearing & Camp Mechanics

### Efficient Clearing Techniques
- **Full clear as default**: In most situations, especially when uncertain or in lower ELO, prioritize a full clear of your jungle camps rather than looking for early ganks. This provides consistent gold and XP income.
- **Consistent pathing**: Clear camps in a reliable order (typically top to bot or bot to top) to establish rhythm and predict camp respawn timers.
- **Camp synchronization**: Clear camps in a way that they respawn simultaneously. For example, when playing Wukong, clear wolves first to ensure all camps respawn at optimal times for your next clear.
- **Objective-adjacent clearing**: Before objectives spawn, clear camps on the opposite side of the map from where the objective will appear to maintain map presence and avoid being caught out.
- **Post-kill clearing**: After securing a kill or objective, immediately clear your camps before basing if you need to maintain tempo and map pressure.

### Kiting Camps and Aggro Management
- **Wall interaction**: For champions like Graves, hooking terrain walls can cancel animation locks (like E) to enable faster camp clearing and repositioning.
- **Positioning**: Maintain optimal positioning during clears to minimize damage taken from monsters while maximizing damage output through ability resets and auto-attacks.
- **Pet management**: For champions with jungle pets (like Rengar), understand your pet's damage output and timing to maximize clear efficiency—e.g., knowing when your pet can finish a camp so you can move to the next immediately.

### Ability Usage for Clearing
- **Auto-attack reset**: Time abilities to reset auto-attack animations for faster clearing (e.g., Lee Sin's Q after auto-attack, Rengar's Q for empowered auto).
- **Resource management**: 
  - Graves: Maintain 6-8 E stacks for maximum damage output (each E adds extra damage); 8 stacks allow true damage of 87/sec, enabling clearing two camps simultaneously.
  - General: Use smite on large monsters (buffs, epic monsters) to secure them and reduce clear time.
- **Ability sequencing**: 
  - Lee Sin: Start with E to enable a full clear path.
  - Lillia: Use Q-auto-auto sequence followed by smite for efficient camp clearing.
  - Rengar: Utilize empowered Q (from Ferocity) for burst damage on large monsters.

### Clear Speed Optimization
- **Item impact**: Early items like Long Sword significantly increase clear speed—prioritize these when gold allows.
- **Stack-dependent champions**: For champions with stacking mechanics (Graves, Senna), focus on maintaining maximum stacks during clears to exponentially increase damage output.
- **Path efficiency**: 
  - Clear camps in minimal movement paths (e.g., raptors → gromp → blue buff for Lillia invade paths).
  - Avoid unnecessary detours between camps.
- **Tempo awareness**: "Every second that you're faster on the map might be a free kill." Optimize clears to gain tempo advantages over the enemy jungler.
- **HP preservation**: Efficient clearing isn't just about speed—it's about clearing while maintaining high HP to enable immediate ganking or invading afterward.

### Camp Tracking and Timing
- **Post-3-camp check**: After clearing your first three camps, immediately assess whether the enemy jungler can level-3 gank your lanes based on their pathing and your lane states.
- **Enemy jungler prediction**: Constantly track enemy jungler's camp clears to predict their location—e.g., if they started top side, they'll likely be on bot side after their second clear.
- **Respawn timing awareness**: Know exact camp respawn timers (typically 2:00-2:30 for standard camps) to time your clears for maximum efficiency and to predict when enemy camps will be available for counter-jungling.
- **Wave state integration**: Combine camp timing with lane wave states—e.g., if you know enemy jungler will finish their clear in 20 seconds, check if allied lanes will be in a gankable state at that time.
- **Jungle tracking discipline**: After every camp clear, ask: "What can the enemy jungler do right now?" This informs your next move (gank, farm, invade, or base).

### When to Skip Camps vs. When to Clear
- **Skip extra camps when down tempo**: After taking an objective or kill, if you're behind on tempo (enemy can move first), base immediately rather than taking additional camps—e.g., "if you're stronger and you take one extra camp when enemy takes Herald, you can't contest."
- **Clear before basing when ahead**: If you have gold advantage and need to itemize, clear your camps first to maintain tempo, then base—e.g., "I'm not going to base because I want to full clear all my camps so they respawn at the same time, then I'll base."
- **Prioritize camps when behind**: When behind in gold/XP, focus on clearing camps to catch up rather than forcing low-percentage ganks.
- **Skip camps for objectives**: When an objective is spawning soon and you need to contest it, skip clearing distant camps to arrive at the objective with full health and resources.
- **Skip camps when lanes need help**: If allied lanes are in immediate danger and you have tempo advantage, skip your next clear to provide assistance—e.g., pinging lanes to play safe while you gank.

### Base Timers and Their Importance
- **Win probability correlation**: "Having very good base timers allows you to have a higher probability of winning fights and be as strong as possible."
- **Base triggers**:
  - Low HP during or after fights (you can't fight effectively when low).
  - Gold threshold for item power spikes (e.g., 800-1200 gold for core items).
  - Before objective spawns (to be at full strength for contests).
  - When enemy jungler has tempo advantage (to reset and avoid being caught out).
- **Base efficiency**: 
  - Base instantly when needed—don't delay by clearing extra camps first.
  - Time your base arrival to coincide with camp respawns so you return to a freshly cleared jungle.
  - Use base time to purchase items that significantly impact clear speed (e.g., Long Sword for early clear, Mythic items for mid-game).
- **Tempo management**: Proper base timers prevent camping downtime—"if you die, enemy knows where you are and you'll be back in base, meaning your camps won't respawn, putting you far behind in gold and XP."

### Common Mistakes
- **Taking extra camps when down tempo**: Clearing additional camps after an objective when you should be basing to contest the next objective or assist lanes.
- **Ignoring camp timers for ganks**: Forgoing camp clears to chase low-percentage ganks, resulting in lost gold/XP and falling behind.
- **Poor pathing causing camp desync**: Clearing camps in inefficient orders that cause them to respawn at staggered times, creating downtime between clears.
- **Not tracking enemy jungler**: Failing to monitor enemy jungler's position and clear speed, leading to unsafe invades or missed counter-jungle opportunities.
- **Basing too early/late**: 
  - Basing with full health and no item needs, wasting map pressure.
  - Delaying base when low on HP or needing items, dying unnecessarily in fights or missing power spikes.
- **Over-reliance on ganking**: Forcing ganks on strong lanes or when enemy jungler has priority, resulting in deaths and lost tempo.
- **Neglecting HP preservation**: Taking unnecessary damage during clears that prevents immediate ganking or invading afterward.
- **Misjudging lane states for ganks**: Attempting ganks on lanes with healthy waves or vision when the enemy jungler is nearby and can counter-gank.

### Quick Reference
- **Clear speed is tempo**: Every second faster in clearing creates potential kill opportunities—prioritize efficient clears over forced ganks.
- **Sync your camps**: Clear in patterns that cause camps to respawn simultaneously to minimize downtime between clears.
- **Always track enemy jungler**: After every camp clear, ask "What can the enemy jungler do right now?" to inform your next decision.
- **Base for power spikes, not habit**: Base only when low HP, needing an item for an objective, or to reset tempo—never base just because you finished a clear.
- **Skip extra camps when behind on tempo**: After objectives/kills, if enemy can move first, base immediately rather than taking additional camps.
