##Late Game & Teamfighting

### Baron/Elder Dance Fundamentals
- **Tank Check First**: Before attempting Baron/Elder, verify if your team can tank it. If not, abandon the attempt immediately—enemy team will collapse and kill you.
- **Force Engagement**: If you can tank it, check if you can drop to ~3K HP to force enemy team to come fight you. This creates a 5v5 scenario where you control the engagement.
- **Engagement Options When Enemy Comes**:
  - **Turn and Fight**: If you believe you can win the teamfight (consider ultimates, items, numbers), engage immediately when they commit.
  - **Finish and Zone**: If you're ahead but unsure about winning a fight, finish the objective, zone the enemy jungler, then either retreat or fight after based on the outcome.
- **Disengage When Necessary**: If you cannot force the enemy to come (can't drop to sufficient HP), back off immediately (press B)—forcing a Baron/Elder without threat is suicide.
- **Walking Timings**: 
  - Base to Baron/Elder: 16-17 seconds standard
  - With homeguards: ~13 seconds
  - Without proper boots: 20-22 seconds
  - With dash champions: Can be significantly faster (factor in your mobility)
- **Pre-Objective Preparation**: Always base first to ensure full items/health before attempting Baron/Elder. Never start it while low on resources.

### Objective Control & Vision
- **River Control Wave**: Push the first wave before attempting Baron/Elder to establish river control and force enemy laners to respond.
- **Vision Denial**: 
  - Clear all wards (especially control wards) around the objective before starting it.
  - Place a pink ward on the objective pit—this expires enemy blue trinkets on contact.
  - If you have pink ward and enemy uses blue trinket, their vision will expire in ~8-10 seconds.
- **Hide-and-Seek Tactic**:
  1. Push wave to establish lane priority
  2. Clear all vision around objective
  3. Walk toward objective (do not stand in bush after clearing wards—lingering vision remains)
  4. Bait enemy into face-checking by appearing to commit
  5. Collapse on them when they engage
- **Communication Protocol**: 
  - Ping teammates who move toward objective too early ("Assist me" ping on bush)
  - Use clear, timely pings to coordinate hide-and-seek execution
  - Call out when enemy vision is denied ("Their vision is down")
- **Vision Trapping**: 
  - Hit enemy blue trinket to make them see you
  - Hit Baron/Elder for 2 seconds, then leave immediately to trap over-aggressive enemies
  - Only commit fully when you have vision advantage or enemy is committed elsewhere

### Teamfight Positioning & Engagement
- **Objective as Bait**: Baron/Elder's primary value is forcing enemy engagement—if they don't come to contest, you can take it safely with minimal risk.
- **Steal Attempts**: Enemy 3v5 steal attempts on Baron/Elder almost always fail—your team should collapse immediately and win the ensuing fight.
- **Losing Team Strategy**: When behind, avoid fighting at the objective. Instead, look for teamfights in mid lane where you have better numbers and escape routes.
- **Winning Team Strategy**: When ahead, use vision control to bait enemies into poor engages at the objective, then punish with superior numbers.
- **Post-Fight Objective**: After winning a teamfight near Baron/Elder, you can safely take it—enemy must respawn or concede.
- **Cooldown Awareness**: 
  - Always check enemy ultimate timers before committing to fights
  - If enemy has all ultimates available, they may base instantly and look to fight through mid lane
  - Delay engages if your key ultimates are down unless you have overwhelming advantage
- **Overstay Punishment**: 
  - Collapse on laners who overstay in lane (e.g., pushing too deep without vision)
  - This is especially effective after 14 minutes when tower dive damage increases significantly
- **Herald/Elder Priority**: 
  - If mid tower is up and Herald/Elder is spawning, almost always play for the objective to break mid tower
  - General rule: When mid tower is up, prioritize Herald/Elder if contestable
  - If no Herald/Elder up, play mid lane into the side lane tower that's still up to force enemy to catch wave

### Peel & Protect Strategies
- **Carry Protection**: Use Baron/Elder threat to zone enemy engages—position between enemy engages and your carries.
- **Peel Focus**: Prioritize stopping enemy engages on your carries over chasing fleeing enemies. Your value is in keeping your damage dealers alive.
- **Terrain Utilization**: 
  - Force enemies through narrow paths (river entrances, jungle choke points) when peeling
  - Use terrain to split enemy teams during engages
- **Communication**: 
  - Clearly assign peel responsibilities ("I'll zone their engage, you follow up on their backline")
  - Call out when enemy engages are coming ("Their engage is coming in 3 seconds")
- **Counter-Peel**: If enemy is diving your backline, consider counter-engaging on their frontline instead of purely reacting—this can turn the fight in your favor.

### Common Mistakes
- **Starting Baron/Elder without vision control**: Attempting objectives while enemy has vision control almost always results in steals or lost teamfights.
- **Ignoring lingering vision**: Standing in bushes after clearing wards gets you picked off by enemy vision that hasn't faded yet.
- **Forcing fights without advantage**: Engaging at Baron/Elder when enemy has equal/better ultimates, numbers, or positioning.
- **Overcommitting to steals**: Trying to steal Baron/Elder 3v5 when behind—this consistently loses the fight and the objective.
- **Neglecting wave state**: Not pushing waves before objectives, giving enemy laners free roam to collapse.
- **Poor communication**: Failing to ping teammates who move too early or not calling out vision control status.
- **Basing too late**: Waiting until low HP/items to base before Baron/Elder attempts, reducing your threat level.
- **Misjudging timings**: Not accounting for walking time to objective, leading to late engages or missed opportunities.
- **Fighting in enemy territory**: Attempting teamfights in enemy jungle when behind instead of pulling to your own side of the map.
- **Overlooking tower dive power**: Not taking advantage of increased tower dive damage after 14 minutes to create pressure.

### Quick Reference
- **Baron/Elder Decision Flow**: 
  1. Can you tank it? (If no → back off) 
  2. Can you drop to ~3K HP? (If yes → force engage) 
  3. If enemy comes → turn and fight OR finish and zone based on fight winnability
- **Vision Control Non-Negotiables**: 
  - Clear all wards + place pink ward on objective before attempting 
  - Push wave first for river control 
  - Never stand in bush after clearing wards (walk to bait)
- **Critical Timings**: 
  - Base to Baron/Elder: 16-17s (adjust for boots/dashes) 
  - Pink ward expires enemy blue trinket: ~8-10s 
  - Tower dives become significantly easier: after 14 minutes
- **Objective Priority**: 
  - Mid tower up + Herald/Elder spawning → almost always play for objective 
  - No objective up → play mid lane into standing side tower to force enemy wave catch
- **Teamfight Principle**: 
  - Baron/Elder is a baiting tool—if enemy doesn't come to contest, take it safely 
  - Losing teams: fight in mid lane, not at objective 
  - Winning teams: use vision to bait enemies into poor engages at objective
