## Jungle Fundamentals

### Core Concepts and Mindset

#### The What, How, When Framework
- Always ask: **What** are you playing for? (objective/teammate), **How** will you play for it, **When** will you play for it
- After every kill or objective, immediately reassess: "What am I playing for next and when?"
- Identify your win condition early: which lanes/objectives lead to victory based on team composition
- Play through mid lane when possible (after minute 14, middle waves are faster than side lanes)
- Secure lane push first before taking objectives (e.g., push mid lane before taking Herald)

#### Champion Identity and Tempo
- Understand your champion's identity: 
  - Level 2/3 gankers (Lee Sin, Elise): look for early opportunities but don't force
  - Farm-to-6 champions (Fiddlesticks, Nasus): prioritize farming unless free kill presents
- High tempo is non-negotiable: optimize clear speed, pathing efficiency, and HP preservation
- Every second faster on the map creates potential kill opportunities
- **Never die unnecessarily**: dying loses tempo, gives enemy information, and prevents camp respawns
- Stay alive to maintain map pressure and item advantage

#### Information Gathering and Tracking
- **Ward level 1 (after 1:00)**: Spot enemy jungler start to predict pathing
- Track enemy jungler type: 
  - Adapters (start at buffs, jump walls): likely to invade or take opposite buff
  - Farmers: likely to full clear their side
- After finishing your third camp (~2:20-2:30), think: "What can enemy jungler do now?"
- Monitor enemy jungler's respawn timer to anticipate their next move
- Ping laners to play safe based on enemy jungler's potential actions (not just following pings blindly)

### Decision-Making Frameworks

#### Wave States and Gank Timing
- **Perfect gank timing**: when enemy laner is going for last hit (CS) - they're not looking at minimap
- **Wave state analysis**:
  - Slow push **towards you**: enemy is vulnerable (must stay to last hit)
  - Slow push **towards enemy**: enemy is safe (can retreat to tower)
  - Bouncing wave: ~1 minute until crash - enemy must be at wave to last hit
- **Gank timer**: Enemy jungler takes ~5-7 seconds to walk from camp to mid lane after clearing
- Higher elo players react faster to ganks (adjust timing accordingly)

#### Gank Execution Checklist
Before ganking, verify:
1. **Wave state**: Is enemy laner in vulnerable position? (slow push towards them)
2. **Enemy vision**: Are there wards in the area? (no wards = safe approach)
3. **Your CC**: Should go first (deeper in lane = more damage time)
4. **Damage output**: Your damage + laner damage = kill potential
5. **Enemy jungle proximity**: Can they respond in time? (check respawn timer)
- **Do not force ganks** if conditions aren't met - wait for better opportunities
- When ganking: 
  - Approach from behind to cut off escape path
  - Hold abilities until enemy uses key escapes (flash, dash)
  - Walk on map edges/walls to avoid vision
  - Ping the objective after getting enemy's attention (not on the way)

#### Base Timers and Resource Management
**Base when**:
- Low HP (cannot fight effectively)
- You have an item power spike ready to buy (600+ gold for core item)
- Camps are far away (>15 seconds walk from current position)
- You're on path to your win condition (need to be in position)
- An objective is about to spawn (need to be strong to contest)
- After taking camps/objectives (to spend gold before making plays)

**Base timers**:
- **25 second rule**: Base 25 seconds before camp spawns (8 seconds to base + ~17 seconds to walk from base to camp)
- After base, immediately think: "What am I playing for?"
- Clear camps on the way to your win condition/objective
- **Never walk through camps with low HP and gold** - base instantly instead
- After taking objective: base if you need items, then reassess win condition

#### Jungler Tracking and Communication
- **Your job**: Know what enemy jungler can do and communicate it to laners
- After your actions (clear, gank, objective), ask: "What can enemy jungler do now?"
- Ping laners to be careful based on enemy jungler's potential path (not just reacting)
- Communicate enemy jungler's potential actions **before** they happen (proactive pinging)
- When not doing anything active, constantly think: "What can enemy team do right now?"
- **Do not follow teammate pings blindly** - you have the bigger picture as jungler
- Only help losing lanes if it doesn't cost you tempo or map control (unless absolutely necessary)

### Advanced Concepts

#### Lane Purity and Snowballing
- **Abuse lane purity**: When enemy laner is pushed in under tower, they're vulnerable to ganks
- **Maintain lead before snowballing**: 
  1. Generate lead (early kills/objectives)
  2. Maintain lead (jungler tracking, base timers, avoiding deaths)
  3. Only then snowball lead (invade enemy jungle, aggressive plays)
- When ahead, prioritize maintaining your lead over aggressive plays
- Use enemy's "turn" (time after they take objective/camp) to invade their jungle

#### Objective Control
- Before objective spawn: base to get strongest items possible
- After taking objective: 
  1. Immediately think "what am I playing for next?"
  2. Base if needed for next power spike
  3. Reassess win condition based on new map state
- Take enemy camps before objectives **only if you're winning** (can always take objective later)
- Three camps give more XP than one kill - prioritize farming over low-percentage plays

### Common Mistakes

- **Forcing ganks without checking conditions**: ignoring wave state, enemy vision, or jungle proximity
- **Walking through camps with low HP and gold** instead of basing instantly
- **Not reassessing win condition** after kills/objectives (just continuing old plan)
- **Following teammate pings blindly** without considering enemy jungler's potential
- **Failing to ping laners proactively** about enemy jungler threats (only reacting after damage)
- **Overcommitting to losing lanes** and losing tempo/map control unnecessarily
- **Skipping camps to make low-percentage plays** when three camps give more XP and map pressure
- **Not tracking enemy jungler respawn timer** leading to surprise ganks or invasions
- **Baseing too late** (missing the 25-second window before camp spawns)
- **Playing for the wrong win condition** based on outdated game state
- **Not cutting off escape paths** when ganking (allowing enemy to flee safely)
- **Using ultimate too early** in skirmishes when saving it for objective fight is better
- **Ignoring lane purity** - missing gank opportunities when enemies are pushed in
- **Snowballing before maintaining lead** - throwing away advantages with aggressive plays

### Quick Reference

1. **What, How, When**: After every kill/objective, immediately reassess what you're playing for next and when
2. **Wave State Ganks**: Only gank when enemy laner is going for last hit (CS) - they're vulnerable and not watching minimap
3. **25-Second Base Rule**: Base 25 seconds before camp spawns to hit item timers perfectly
4. **Jungler Tracking Priority**: Your main job is predicting enemy jungler's next move and warning laners **before** it happens
5. **Three Camps > One Kill**: Prioritize full clears and base timers over low-percentage plays - three camps give more XP and map pressure than a single kill
