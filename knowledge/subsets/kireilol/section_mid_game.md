## Mid Game Macro (Levels 6-14)

### Transition from Early to Mid Game
- Around minute 14, mid lane wave speed becomes faster than side lane waves, making mid lane the primary rotation path (XeDz45ySYIU)
- Mid game purity is everything: If you can play through mid lane effectively, always prioritize it over side lanes (XeDz45ySYIU)
- Early game focuses on "what" and "when"; mid game requires adding "how" to your decision framework (XeDz45ySYIU)
- Transition timing varies by game state but typically begins after first turret falls and before first major objective spawn (Herald/Baron)

### Power Spikes and Item Timing
- Base instantly when you have sufficient gold for a core power spike item (z2_In7-gR0Q, _QPYvnzKxY8)
- Power spike items dramatically increase fight potential – never fight without them when you have the gold (6fiaw8CKBQE)
- After acquiring a power spike item, base to get strong before looking for plays (GrlYItFK0zk, 91NnpZLn_Fs)
- Example timings: Kraken Slayer (~1.3k gold), Eclipse (~1.4k gold) – know your champion's specific power spike (z2_In7-gR0Q)
- When ahead: Base for items before objectives to maximize your strength advantage (6fiaw8CKBQE)
- When behind: Prioritize base timers to close item gaps rather than forcing fights

### Grouping vs Split Decisions
- **Always play through mid lane when winning** – creates fastest rotations and map pressure (XeDz45ySYIU, 1N2t85s_AXs)
- **Ping Pong Strategy Requirements:**
  1. One winning lane capable of pushing wave and moving with jungler
  2. Jungler must clear camps before rotating
- **Execution:** Winning lane pushes → creates man advantage → rotates to help other lanes (1N2t85s_AXs)
- **Side laner responsibility:** Play safe until winning lane moves together, then rotate to create numbers advantage (1N2t85s_AXs)
- **Jungler responsibility:** Clear camps, then move with winning lane to enable rotations (1N2t85s_AXs)
- When ahead: Group for objectives and side lane pressure (Q8KQWF_qIG8)
- When behind: Play defensively, look for picks rather than forcing 5v5 fights (Q8KQWF_qIG8)

### Tempo Management
- **Tempo Definition:** Who can move first on the map (5pqfQUd_qbU)
- **Tempo Types:**
  * Jungle down: Jungler farming/basing → laners play safe
  * Objective down: After taking objective → must push wave/base before moving
  * Base down: When basing for items → enemy gains first move opportunity
  * Death Tempo: Enemy died and respawning with home guards
- **Measuring Tempo:** Death timers + base time + healing time (5pqfQUd_qbU)
- **When Down Tempo:** Play safe, don't push wave unnecessarily, ping laners to be careful (5pqfQUd_qbU)
- **When Up Tempo:** Look for plays, group for objectives, invade enemy jungle (5pqfQUd_qbU)
- **Critical Insight:** "If you're stronger as a jungler you should instantly base and not take one extra Camp because if you're stronger and you take let's say one extra Camp if enemy R The Herald you can't contest" (5pqfQUd_qbU)

### What/How/When Decision Framework
- **Always ask three questions after any kill/objective/base:**
  1. **What** are you playing for? (Objective, turret, lane pressure)
  2. **How** are you playing for it? (Which wave, which path)
  3. **When** are you playing for it? (Wave timing, enemy respawn timers) (XeDz45ySYIU)
- **What:** Usually connected to an objective and enabling teammates (XeDz45ySYIU)
- **How:** Depends on wave timing – must be on correct wave to contest effectively (XeDz45ySYIU)
- **When:** Based on wave spawns and your camp clearing speed (XeDz45ySYIU)
- **Immediate Post-Action Thought:** After any kill/objective/base, instantly think "what am I playing for next and with who" (XeDz45ySYIU, QNwt2GhKVoY)
- **Wave Calculation Example:** To play on a wave spawning from base, calculate how many camps you can clear to arrive on time (XeDz45ySYIU)

### Objective Control (Herald/Baron)
- **Herald Priority:** If mid tower is up, almost always play for Herald (ZBKr-SEuX70)
  * Herald enables mid tower pressure which opens side lane opportunities
  * After Herald: Push mid lane wave first for river control before rotating (Q8KQWF_qIG8)
- **Baron Execution Protocol:**
  1. **First wave push:** Always for river control (especially mid lane) (7dx3pdLTvjg, QNwt2GhKVoY)
  2. **Critical vision control:** Clear ALL wards around Baron before attempting (7dx3pdLTvjg)
  3. **Vision denial:** Ensure enemy vision is black around objective area
  4. **Baiting:** Walk toward objective to bait enemy face-checks
  5. **Hide and seek:** After clearing wards, hide and wait for enemy to face-check (QNwt2GhKVoY)
  6. **Communication:** Ping teammates when committing to contest (7dx3pdLTvjg)
  7. **Post-Baron:** Push wave again for second objective pressure (QNwt2GhKVoY)
- **Baron Decision Factors:**
  1. Can you tank Baron? (If not, don't take it) (r9KJiLMCy4Q)
  2. Can you drop to 3K HP to force enemy to come fight? (If yes, you can zone them) (r9KJiLMCy4Q)
  3. If enemy gives range → turn and fight; if no range → finish Baron and zone (r9KJiLMCy4Q)
- **Baron Timing:** Typically 16-17 seconds with proper setup and vision control (r9KJiLMCy4Q)

### Base Timing and Pathing
- **Base when:**
  1. Low HP (cannot fight effectively) (z2_In7-gR0Q, _QPYvnzKxY8)
  2. Need item power spike (z2_In7-gR0Q, _QPYvnzKxY8, 6fiaw8CKBQE)
  3. Camps are far away (base is more time efficient than walking) (z2_In7-gR0Q)
  4. On path to win condition (need to be in position for next play) (z2_In7-gR0Q)
  5. Objective about to spawn (need maximum strength) (z2_In7-gR0Q, 6fiaw8CKBQE)
- **Critical Timing:** Bot lane to blue side takes 15-16 seconds – base is often faster than walking (z2_In7-gR0Q)
- **Post-Base Principle:** Always think "what am I playing for" before moving out from base (_QPYvnzKxY8)
- **Pathing Example:** After getting item, go from top to bot (or bot to top) to position for your win condition (6fiaw8CKBQE)
- **Specific Callout:** "If you kill ball in here okay and let's say all your camps are up okay and you have a lot of gold and you're low you should base here and go from top to Bol" (6fiaw8CKBQE)

### Wave Management and Lane States
- **Wave States:** Slow push, normal, fast push (3TiClwpBHuo, HjLP-LlYpvg)
- **Slow Push Identification:** Wave stacking under tower, vulnerable to ganks when it starts pushing out (3TiClwpBHuo)
- **Key Wave Insight:** "If you understand waves you actually understand how to get kills if you understand waves that means the enemy has to overextend to basically in okay that's a thing" (3TiClwpBHuo)
- **Ganking Timing:** "One minute till it crash on your timer" for slow push waves (3TiClwpBHuo)
- **Lane Volatility:** Recognize which lanes will be volatile before game starts (HjLP-LlYpvg)
- **Wave Bouncing:** When wave bounces toward enemy → safe for laners to farm (HjLP-LlYpvg)
- **Wave Crashing:** When wave about to crash on enemy tower → laners vulnerable to ganks (HjLP-LlYpvg)
- **Critical Pattern:** "Every single time a wave is slow push toward enemy it is a chance for enemy jungler to gank" (HjLP-LlYpvg)

### Communication and Vision Control
- **Minimap Discipline:** Constantly check minimap for enemy jungler positioning and lane states (-I-S7cbFrKc)
- **Post-3rd Camp Protocol:** After clearing third camp, think "what can enemy jungler do?" and ping laners accordingly (-I-S7cbFrKc)
- **Core Communication Principle:** "Ping laners to be careful" when:
  * You're not with them (jungler farming/basing)
  * Enemy jungler is missing from map
  * You're taking objectives or basing
- **Jungler Tracking Responsibility:** "Your job as jungler is recognize he has one turn to make one play" (6fiaw8CKBQE)
- **Specific Ping Examples:**
  * "Ping can you word for me ping him in his face ping what you want" (BCJHRhrxbH8)
  * "Ping your laners hey be careful" when enemy jungler is nearby (-I-S7cbFrKc)
  * Ping laners to play safe when you're clearing camps or basing (multiple sources)
- **Vision Control:** Always clear wards around objectives before contesting (7dx3pdLTvjg)
- **Objective Safety:** Never start Baron/Herdar without first pushing wave for river control and clearing enemy vision (7dx3pdLTvjg)
