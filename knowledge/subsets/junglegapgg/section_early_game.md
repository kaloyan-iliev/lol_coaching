## Early Game (Levels 1-6)

### First Clear Decisions
- **Clear speed and camp order set your tempo.** A fast, structured clear lets you be back at your Grump when it spawns unless you have a guaranteed play.  
- **Standard vs. mirrored clears.** A full clear (e.g., Red → Raptors → Krugs → Gromp → Wolves → Blue) is safe; mirroring the enemy’s start (e.g., taking Raptors then Krugs if they began Raptors) often yields a smoother second clear.  
- **Jungle tracking at camp 3.** When you finish your third camp, glance at the minimap to see where the enemy jungler is on his third camp. If he’s also on his third camp, you know his pathing direction and can anticipate his next move (e.g., “Hecrim will go bot”).  
- **Invade timing based on enemy start.** If you see the enemy began Raptors, expect his Raptors to respawn ~2:00–2:15 and consider an invade after your clear if you have vision or a numbers advantage.  
- **Base timers and Smite efficiency.** Base when your blue is spawning and you have no other immediate objective. One‑shot camps with Smite (don’t hold it) – this saves roughly 10 seconds per clear and keeps your tempo high.  
- **When in doubt, farm.** If you lack a clear, high‑percentage play (invade, gank, or objective), default to farming your own camps rather than forcing a low‑percentage risk.

### Early Gank Windows
- **Level 2–3:** First realistic gank window after securing your first buff and hitting level 2. Look for lanes with hard CC or enemy summoner‑spell cooldowns.  
- **Level 3 power spike (double buffs).** After Red + Blue you have stronger dueling and 2v2/3v3 potential. Prioritize ganks where the enemy laner is missing Flash or is overextended.  
- **Level 4 power spike.** Usually reached after your first clear + Scuttle or after a second camp. Use the extra ability/stat boost to contest the first Scuttle, dive a lane with a summoner‑spell disadvantage, or look for a 2v2 skirmish.  
- **Use spare time for information.** After a clear, walk to ward enemy jungle entrances or check the minimap/chat for Flash, Heal, or Ult cooldowns before committing to a gank.  
- **High‑percentage gank rule.** Only gank when you have a numbers advantage, the enemy lacks a key summoner spell (especially Flash), or your lane provides hard CC that guarantees follow‑up.  
- **If no guaranteed play exists,** revert to farming or contesting the nearest Scuttle rather than forcing a risky dive.

### Scuttle Crab
- **First Scuttle (~2:00).** Contest only if you have vision or a numbers advantage; otherwise, give it up and use the time to farm or invade the enemy’s weak side.  
- **Second Scuttle (~3:15).** This spawn is a major tempo swing. If you have a level or numbers lead (e.g., you’re level 4 vs. enemy level 3), you can often win it outright.  
- **Vision on Dragon pit.** When fighting for Scuttle, keep vision on the Dragon pit. Giving up Dragon for a Scuttle is rarely worth it unless you have a clear follow‑up plan (e.g., a guaranteed gank).  
- **If you lose the Scuttle,** do not over‑extend for Dragon; instead, reset to your own camps or look for a counter‑gank on the opposite side.  
- **Use spare time after a clear** to walk to the nearest Scuttle and secure it with Smite; this often yields a better return than wandering aimlessly.

### Level 3/4 Power Spikes
- **Level 3 (double buffs).** Provides increased damage and durability. Look for 2v2/3v3 skirmishes where you can flank or for lanes where the enemy has no Flash.  
- **Level 4 (after first clear + Scuttle or second camp).** Unlocks your second basic ability or gives a stronger all‑in. Use it to contest the first Dragon, secure a Scuttle, or dive a lane with a summoner‑spell disadvantage.  
- **Track enemy level via camp timers.** If you see the enemy still on his second camp while you are on your third, you have a tempo advantage and can play more aggressively.  
- **Weak early‑game champs (e.g., Shyvana, Nasus).** Prioritize farming and jungle tracking over early ganks; wait for your level 6 power spike.  
- **Level 6 spike.** When your ultimate is up and the enemy laner has no Flash, you can often guarantee a kill. Communicate this to your lane (“enemy bot no flash, I have ult at 6”) and collapse for the kill.

### Common Mistakes
- **Neglecting jungle tracker:** walking into enemy invades or missing gank chances because you didn’t note the enemy’s camp timer.  
- **Holding Smite unnecessarily:** losing ~10 seconds each clear by not one‑shotting camps.  
- **Forcing ganks/Scuttles without vision, numbers, or summoner‑spell advantage:** leading to avoidable deaths.  
- **Over‑extending for Dragon after losing a Scuttle:** allowing the enemy jungler to vacuum your side of the jungle.  
- **Poor mental stack:** chatting, being on Discord, or not clearing distractions, which reduces information processing and leads to bad decisions.  
- **Wasting spare time:** idling or wandering after a clear instead of using it to ward, check summoner spells, or farm information.  
- **Basing at the wrong time:** recalling too early (missing farm) or too late (losing tempo); base only when you have nothing else to do (e.g., blue spawning) and can do so safely.  
- **Taking Dragon without vision or follow‑up:** giving the enemy jungler free rein to steal your camps while you’re committed elsewhere.

### Quick Reference
- **Clear fast, be back at Grump on spawn unless you have a guaranteed play.**  
- **Track enemy jungle:** after your third camp, note his third‑camp position to predict his path.  
- **One‑shot camps with Smite (~10 s saved per clear); base when blue is spawning and you have nothing else to do.**  
- **Contest Scuttle only with vision or numbers; the second Scuttle (~3:15) is a major tempo swing.**  
- **Look for level‑3 (double buffs) and level‑4 spikes to gank when enemy lacks Flash or is overextended.**  
- **When in doubt, farm your own camps rather than risking low‑percentage plays.**  
- **Communicate flash‑advantage opportunities (e.g., “enemy bot no flash, I have ult at 6”) and collapse with your lane.**
