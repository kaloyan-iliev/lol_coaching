## Clearing & Camp Mechanics

### Core Clearing Techniques
- Pull camps during every auto attack: Direction your movement toward the next camp while attacking (e.g., while hitting Gromp, pull it along your path to Wolves)
- Kiting toward next camp: Instead of walking straight to camps, guide them in your travel direction to eliminate transition time between camps
- Optimize camp completion points: Finish each camp at a location that positions you immediately for the next camp (e.g., end Gromp where you can walk straight to Wolves without repositioning)
- Utilize full leash range: Maximize camp pull distance while moving to minimize idle walking between auto attacks
- Champion-specific optimization: Even slow-clearing champions (like Jax) must mechanics-minmax their clear through precise pulling and kiting

### Tempo Management Framework
- **Camps Up vs Camps Down** (universal coach consensus):
  - When YOUR camps are alive (up): Only execute actions with ≥90% success certainty (guaranteed kills, safe objectives, high-percentage ganks)
  - When YOUR camps are dead (down): Free time for information gathering, invasive plays, or lower-percentage opportunities
- Base timing: Recall immediately after full clear to reset timers and avoid walking back wasted time
- Spare time allocation: Use camps-down periods for:
  - Sweeping lanes for vision control
  - Checking enemy jungler position via mini map/chat
  - Low-risk counter-jungling when enemy location known
  - Objective control only when vision-secured and camp timers allow

### Information Gathering During Clear
- Click and pan technique: When moving between camps, click your destination and pan camera toward nearby lanes (prioritizing closest lanes) to gather info without losing clear speed
- Intentional camera panning: Before panning, define exactly what information you need (e.g., "Where is enemy jungler after my blue?")
- Mini map discipline: Continuously track enemy jungler icons, health bars, and lane states during clear
- Chat monitoring: Use chat history (V key bind) to track enemy summoner spell usage and pings for decision-making
- Lane proximity priority: When panning, focus first on lanes nearest to your current path (e.g., bot lane when between Krugs and Raptors)

### Ability Usage & Clear Optimization
- Ability efficiency: Avoid wasting abilities on camps when they could save time (e.g., holding smite for objectives unless camp clear is threatened)
- Pathing awareness: While moving between camps, maintain awareness of enemy jungler's likely position based on tracked pathing
- Post-last camp planning: Before finishing your final camp (Gromp/Krugs/etc.), already know your next action (recall, gank, invade) based on camp state and enemy position
- Base timer precision: Time recalls to arrive at camps exactly as they spawn (e.g., be at Gromp 0.5 seconds before spawn)

### Common Mistakes
- Walking between camps without pulling or kiting (wasting 15-25 seconds per full clear)
- Failing to kite camps toward next destination, repositioning camp leashiting (losing 15-25 seconds per clear)
- Ignoring camp leash range mechanics and walking camps in place
- Ganking or invading when your own camps are up (causing tempo loss)
- Not tracking enemy jungler path during clear, leading to surprise invades
- Wasting camps-down time on indecision instead of information gathering or safe plays
- Taking dragon without vision control when it risks losing multiple camps
- Failing to plan next move after last camp before recalling (causing idle time)
- Arriving late to camp spawns due to poor base timing
- Not using auto attack animations to pull camps (treating them as stationary fights)

### Quick Reference
- Always pull camps during auto attacks toward your next camp destination
- When your camps are up: only take ≥90% certainty plays; when down: use time for information/safe plays
- Employ click and pan between camps for information without sacrificing clear speed
- Base immediately after full clear to maintain tempo and camp timers
- When uncertain about enemy jungle presence: farm your own camps rather than risking invades
