## Vision & Enemy Tracking

### Vision Control and Ward Placement
- Place wards on enemy jungler camps (especially Raptors, Wolves, Gromp) to gain persistent map awareness—this provides "map hacks" until the enemy jungler makes an unusual pathing change (CXv1fuU7tc8)
- Ward key jungle entrances before making high-risk plays (e.g., lane dives) to confirm enemy jungler absence and avoid walking into counter-ganks (H-HS7uHNoXE)
- Prioritize vision control over forcing plays when the enemy team has lane priority, vision advantage, or objective control (f610UsYj5r4)
  - Example: If enemy bot lane has priority, ward, and control, instantly base instead of contesting scuttle or forcing 3v3s
- Use wards to establish enemy jungler's starting side and initial path (e.g., a Raptors ward reveals whether they started red or blue buff) (btOjeZ5Acsk)

### Jungle Tracking Techniques
- **Camp Mirroring**: Track enemy jungler progress by comparing your camp clearance to theirs
  - If enemy jungler is on their third camp when you're on your third camp, they're pathing similarly to you (f610UsYj5r4)
  - If they're pinging aggressive lanes while you're farming, infer their path direction and timing
- **Pathing Deduction**: Use single vision points to predict full routes
  - Seeing enemy jungler at Wolves predicts their next camp (Blue Buff → Raptors → Gromp) (CXv1fuU7tc8)
  - Process of elimination: Spotting them top side means they're not bot side (and vice versa) for the next 60-90 seconds (H-HS7uHNoXE)
  - Note camp timers: Enemy jungler camps spawn at the same time as yours if they started with the same buff
- **Tempo Comparison**: Judge enemy position by comparing clear speeds
  - "I know where he is. I know what his tempo is compared to mine. And I can more or less judge where he's at." (CXv1fuU7tc8)
  - Track how many camps they've cleared relative to you (e.g., if they're one camp ahead, they're likely invading or have a faster clear)

### Predicting Enemy Jungler Movements
- After gaining vision, predict 1-2 moves ahead using standard jungle pathing
  - Example: Seeing enemy jungler at Red Buff → predict Raptors in 20-30 seconds, then Blue Buff (CXv1fuU7tc8)
  - Use respawn timers: Camps respawn at 2:00, 2:15, or 2:30 depending on type—align with enemy's likely path
- **Decision Framework for Ganks/Plays**:
  - **Camps UP** (your camps are active): Only take 90%+ chance ganks, invades, or objective contests (4APtz4zDbGo)
    - Requires confirmed enemy jungler absence (via vision) or high-confidence read (e.g., no flash, low health)
  - **Camps DOWN** (your camps just cleared): Free time for vision control, lower percentage plays, or objective preparation (4APtz4zDbGo)
    - Example: After clearing Krugs, use 20 seconds to deep ward bot lane or prepare for dragon
  - **Prioritize ganks over scuttle** when you know enemy jungler's position and have spare time (4APtz4zDbGo, QQku-iYEQ6k)
    - Example: If you know enemy jungler is taking top scuttle, use spare time to gank mid instead of contesting bot scuttle

### Using Vision Information for Decision Making
- **Pre-Play Assessment**: Before committing to any play, verify enemy jungler's likely position
  - Ward key areas (e.g., river tri-bush) to check for enemy jungler before lane dives or invades (H-HS7uHNoXE)
  - If uncertain, default to safe farming—never walk into a play blind
- **Spare Time Utilization**: Use time between camps effectively based on enemy position
  - If enemy jungler is on opposite side: Deep ward their jungle, prepare for counter-invade, or set up for objective steal
  - If enemy jungler is on your side: Play defensively, ward your jungle entrances, or look for counter-gank opportunities
  - Example: "In my spare time, I'm literally just going to see if this Fizz E on my mid laner." (f610UsYj5r4)
- **Objective Control Timing**: Use vision to time objective contests safely
  - Knowing enemy jungler is doing Krugs gives a 40-50 second window to take dragon uncontested (CXv1fuU7tc8)
  - If enemy jungler shows up at a camp, you know they're not contesting nearby objectives for the next camp clear
- **Lane Safety Communication**: Convert vision into actionable lane warnings
  - Ping specific threats: "I ping where he is [enemy jungler location]. I ping who's in danger [lane]. I ping who is the danger [your champion]" (CXv1fuU7tc8)
  - Never assume laners track enemy jungler—explicitly communicate threats ("Don't assume anything. Laners are very pea-brained.") (CXv1fuU7tc8)

### Tempo Management Based on Enemy Position
- Match enemy jungler's tempo when you have vision advantage
  - If enemy jungler is ahead: Look for aggressive counter-plays (invades, early ganks) to reset tempo
  - If enemy jungler is behind: Play safely to maintain lead, farm efficiently, and deny them resources
- **Base Timing Decisions**:
  - Base when you know enemy jungler is taking an objective you cannot contest (e.g., they're securing dragon while you're low health) (f610UsYj5r4)
  - Do NOT base unnecessarily when vision shows enemy jungler is occupied elsewhere (e.g., clearing opposite jungle)
- **Camp Clear Optimization**:
  - Use camp leash range to pull camps while moving to next camp—every auto attack should pull the camp toward your destination (Nzd24hYQDRo)
  - Avoid walking full distance to camp when you could be pulling it (saves 3-5 seconds per camp)
  - Example: Pulling Gromp toward Blue Buff instead of walking to it then walking away

### Common Mistakes
- Ignoring vision information and relying solely on team pings (e.g., listening to a top dive ping when you know enemy jungler is bot side) (H-HS7uHNoXE)
- Wasting time on dead camps or low-percentage plays when you know enemy jungler's position (Nzd24hYQDRo)
  - Example: Searching for dead scuttle when you know enemy jungler took it and is clearing elsewhere
- Forcing fights without vision advantage or when enemy has lane priority/favorable positioning (f610UsYj5r4)
- Not using spare time effectively after clears (e.g., farming mindlessly instead of warding, sweeping, or adapting) (f610UsYj5r4, QQku-iYEQ6k)
- Assuming enemy jungler position without vision confirmation (CXv1fuU7tc8)
  - "Don't assume anything. [Enemy jungler] can still die here, but you know, some games they will not."
- Neglecting to ping enemy jungler position to help laners (CXv1fuU7tc8)
- Overcommitting to invades or counter-jungling without knowing enemy jungler's current position (btOjeZ5Acsk)
- Failing to adjust playstyle based on whether your camps are up or down (4APtz4zDbGo)

### Quick Reference
- **Ward Early for Map Hacks**: Place a ward on enemy jungler's camps by 1:30 to track their pathing for the entire early game
- **Use Camp Mirroring**: Compare your camp # to enemy jungler's (via vision) to deduce location—e.g., if you're both on camp 2, you're likely pathing similarly
- **Apply Camps Up/Down Framework**:
  - **Camps UP**: Only take 90%+ chance plays (requires vision-confirmed enemy absence)
  - **Camps DOWN**: Free time for vision, invades, or objective prep
- **Post-Play Analysis Habit**: After every play, immediately ask "Where can the enemy jungler be?" and ping specific threats to laners
- **Optimize Spare Time**: Use 15-20 second windows between camps to ward, sweep, or adapt based on known enemy jungler position (never waste this time)
