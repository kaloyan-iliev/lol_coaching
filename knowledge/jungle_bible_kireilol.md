## Jungle Fundamentals

### Core Concepts and Mindset

#### The What, How, When Framework
- Always ask: **What** are you playing for? (objective/teammate), **How** will you play for it, **When** will you play for it
- After every kill or objective, immediately reassess: "What am I playing for next and when?"
- Identify your win condition early: which lanes/objectives lead to victory based on team composition
- Play through mid lane when possible (after minute 14, middle waves are faster than side lanes)
- Secure lane push first before taking objectives (e.g., push mid lane before taking Herald)

#### Champion Identity and Tempo
- Understand your champion's identity: 
  - Level 2/3 gankers (Lee Sin, Elise): look for early opportunities but don't force
  - Farm-to-6 champions (Fiddlesticks, Nasus): prioritize farming unless free kill presents
- High tempo is non-negotiable: optimize clear speed, pathing efficiency, and HP preservation
- Every second faster on the map creates potential kill opportunities
- **Never die unnecessarily**: dying loses tempo, gives enemy information, and prevents camp respawns
- Stay alive to maintain map pressure and item advantage

#### Information Gathering and Tracking
- **Ward level 1 (after 1:00)**: Spot enemy jungler start to predict pathing
- Track enemy jungler type: 
  - Adapters (start at buffs, jump walls): likely to invade or take opposite buff
  - Farmers: likely to full clear their side
- After finishing your third camp (~2:20-2:30), think: "What can enemy jungler do now?"
- Monitor enemy jungler's respawn timer to anticipate their next move
- Ping laners to play safe based on enemy jungler's potential actions (not just following pings blindly)

### Decision-Making Frameworks

#### Wave States and Gank Timing
- **Perfect gank timing**: when enemy laner is going for last hit (CS) - they're not looking at minimap
- **Wave state analysis**:
  - Slow push **towards you**: enemy is vulnerable (must stay to last hit)
  - Slow push **towards enemy**: enemy is safe (can retreat to tower)
  - Bouncing wave: ~1 minute until crash - enemy must be at wave to last hit
- **Gank timer**: Enemy jungler takes ~5-7 seconds to walk from camp to mid lane after clearing
- Higher elo players react faster to ganks (adjust timing accordingly)

#### Gank Execution Checklist
Before ganking, verify:
1. **Wave state**: Is enemy laner in vulnerable position? (slow push towards them)
2. **Enemy vision**: Are there wards in the area? (no wards = safe approach)
3. **Your CC**: Should go first (deeper in lane = more damage time)
4. **Damage output**: Your damage + laner damage = kill potential
5. **Enemy jungle proximity**: Can they respond in time? (check respawn timer)
- **Do not force ganks** if conditions aren't met - wait for better opportunities
- When ganking: 
  - Approach from behind to cut off escape path
  - Hold abilities until enemy uses key escapes (flash, dash)
  - Walk on map edges/walls to avoid vision
  - Ping the objective after getting enemy's attention (not on the way)

#### Base Timers and Resource Management
**Base when**:
- Low HP (cannot fight effectively)
- You have an item power spike ready to buy (600+ gold for core item)
- Camps are far away (>15 seconds walk from current position)
- You're on path to your win condition (need to be in position)
- An objective is about to spawn (need to be strong to contest)
- After taking camps/objectives (to spend gold before making plays)

**Base timers**:
- **25 second rule**: Base 25 seconds before camp spawns (8 seconds to base + ~17 seconds to walk from base to camp)
- After base, immediately think: "What am I playing for?"
- Clear camps on the way to your win condition/objective
- **Never walk through camps with low HP and gold** - base instantly instead
- After taking objective: base if you need items, then reassess win condition

#### Jungler Tracking and Communication
- **Your job**: Know what enemy jungler can do and communicate it to laners
- After your actions (clear, gank, objective), ask: "What can enemy jungler do now?"
- Ping laners to be careful based on enemy jungler's potential path (not just reacting)
- Communicate enemy jungler's potential actions **before** they happen (proactive pinging)
- When not doing anything active, constantly think: "What can enemy team do right now?"
- **Do not follow teammate pings blindly** - you have the bigger picture as jungler
- Only help losing lanes if it doesn't cost you tempo or map control (unless absolutely necessary)

### Advanced Concepts

#### Lane Purity and Snowballing
- **Abuse lane purity**: When enemy laner is pushed in under tower, they're vulnerable to ganks
- **Maintain lead before snowballing**: 
  1. Generate lead (early kills/objectives)
  2. Maintain lead (jungler tracking, base timers, avoiding deaths)
  3. Only then snowball lead (invade enemy jungle, aggressive plays)
- When ahead, prioritize maintaining your lead over aggressive plays
- Use enemy's "turn" (time after they take objective/camp) to invade their jungle

#### Objective Control
- Before objective spawn: base to get strongest items possible
- After taking objective: 
  1. Immediately think "what am I playing for next?"
  2. Base if needed for next power spike
  3. Reassess win condition based on new map state
- Take enemy camps before objectives **only if you're winning** (can always take objective later)
- Three camps give more XP than one kill - prioritize farming over low-percentage plays

### Common Mistakes

- **Forcing ganks without checking conditions**: ignoring wave state, enemy vision, or jungle proximity
- **Walking through camps with low HP and gold** instead of basing instantly
- **Not reassessing win condition** after kills/objectives (just continuing old plan)
- **Following teammate pings blindly** without considering enemy jungler's potential
- **Failing to ping laners proactively** about enemy jungler threats (only reacting after damage)
- **Overcommitting to losing lanes** and losing tempo/map control unnecessarily
- **Skipping camps to make low-percentage plays** when three camps give more XP and map pressure
- **Not tracking enemy jungler respawn timer** leading to surprise ganks or invasions
- **Baseing too late** (missing the 25-second window before camp spawns)
- **Playing for the wrong win condition** based on outdated game state
- **Not cutting off escape paths** when ganking (allowing enemy to flee safely)
- **Using ultimate too early** in skirmishes when saving it for objective fight is better
- **Ignoring lane purity** - missing gank opportunities when enemies are pushed in
- **Snowballing before maintaining lead** - throwing away advantages with aggressive plays

### Quick Reference

1. **What, How, When**: After every kill/objective, immediately reassess what you're playing for next and when
2. **Wave State Ganks**: Only gank when enemy laner is going for last hit (CS) - they're vulnerable and not watching minimap
3. **25-Second Base Rule**: Base 25 seconds before camp spawns to hit item timers perfectly
4. **Jungler Tracking Priority**: Your main job is predicting enemy jungler's next move and warning laners **before** it happens
5. **Three Camps > One Kill**: Prioritize full clears and base timers over low-percentage plays - three camps give more XP and map pressure than a single kill

##Clearing & Camp Mechanics

### Efficient Clearing Techniques
- **Full clear as default**: In most situations, especially when uncertain or in lower ELO, prioritize a full clear of your jungle camps rather than looking for early ganks. This provides consistent gold and XP income.
- **Consistent pathing**: Clear camps in a reliable order (typically top to bot or bot to top) to establish rhythm and predict camp respawn timers.
- **Camp synchronization**: Clear camps in a way that they respawn simultaneously. For example, when playing Wukong, clear wolves first to ensure all camps respawn at optimal times for your next clear.
- **Objective-adjacent clearing**: Before objectives spawn, clear camps on the opposite side of the map from where the objective will appear to maintain map presence and avoid being caught out.
- **Post-kill clearing**: After securing a kill or objective, immediately clear your camps before basing if you need to maintain tempo and map pressure.

### Kiting Camps and Aggro Management
- **Wall interaction**: For champions like Graves, hooking terrain walls can cancel animation locks (like E) to enable faster camp clearing and repositioning.
- **Positioning**: Maintain optimal positioning during clears to minimize damage taken from monsters while maximizing damage output through ability resets and auto-attacks.
- **Pet management**: For champions with jungle pets (like Rengar), understand your pet's damage output and timing to maximize clear efficiency—e.g., knowing when your pet can finish a camp so you can move to the next immediately.

### Ability Usage for Clearing
- **Auto-attack reset**: Time abilities to reset auto-attack animations for faster clearing (e.g., Lee Sin's Q after auto-attack, Rengar's Q for empowered auto).
- **Resource management**: 
  - Graves: Maintain 6-8 E stacks for maximum damage output (each E adds extra damage); 8 stacks allow true damage of 87/sec, enabling clearing two camps simultaneously.
  - General: Use smite on large monsters (buffs, epic monsters) to secure them and reduce clear time.
- **Ability sequencing**: 
  - Lee Sin: Start with E to enable a full clear path.
  - Lillia: Use Q-auto-auto sequence followed by smite for efficient camp clearing.
  - Rengar: Utilize empowered Q (from Ferocity) for burst damage on large monsters.

### Clear Speed Optimization
- **Item impact**: Early items like Long Sword significantly increase clear speed—prioritize these when gold allows.
- **Stack-dependent champions**: For champions with stacking mechanics (Graves, Senna), focus on maintaining maximum stacks during clears to exponentially increase damage output.
- **Path efficiency**: 
  - Clear camps in minimal movement paths (e.g., raptors → gromp → blue buff for Lillia invade paths).
  - Avoid unnecessary detours between camps.
- **Tempo awareness**: "Every second that you're faster on the map might be a free kill." Optimize clears to gain tempo advantages over the enemy jungler.
- **HP preservation**: Efficient clearing isn't just about speed—it's about clearing while maintaining high HP to enable immediate ganking or invading afterward.

### Camp Tracking and Timing
- **Post-3-camp check**: After clearing your first three camps, immediately assess whether the enemy jungler can level-3 gank your lanes based on their pathing and your lane states.
- **Enemy jungler prediction**: Constantly track enemy jungler's camp clears to predict their location—e.g., if they started top side, they'll likely be on bot side after their second clear.
- **Respawn timing awareness**: Know exact camp respawn timers (typically 2:00-2:30 for standard camps) to time your clears for maximum efficiency and to predict when enemy camps will be available for counter-jungling.
- **Wave state integration**: Combine camp timing with lane wave states—e.g., if you know enemy jungler will finish their clear in 20 seconds, check if allied lanes will be in a gankable state at that time.
- **Jungle tracking discipline**: After every camp clear, ask: "What can the enemy jungler do right now?" This informs your next move (gank, farm, invade, or base).

### When to Skip Camps vs. When to Clear
- **Skip extra camps when down tempo**: After taking an objective or kill, if you're behind on tempo (enemy can move first), base immediately rather than taking additional camps—e.g., "if you're stronger and you take one extra camp when enemy takes Herald, you can't contest."
- **Clear before basing when ahead**: If you have gold advantage and need to itemize, clear your camps first to maintain tempo, then base—e.g., "I'm not going to base because I want to full clear all my camps so they respawn at the same time, then I'll base."
- **Prioritize camps when behind**: When behind in gold/XP, focus on clearing camps to catch up rather than forcing low-percentage ganks.
- **Skip camps for objectives**: When an objective is spawning soon and you need to contest it, skip clearing distant camps to arrive at the objective with full health and resources.
- **Skip camps when lanes need help**: If allied lanes are in immediate danger and you have tempo advantage, skip your next clear to provide assistance—e.g., pinging lanes to play safe while you gank.

### Base Timers and Their Importance
- **Win probability correlation**: "Having very good base timers allows you to have a higher probability of winning fights and be as strong as possible."
- **Base triggers**:
  - Low HP during or after fights (you can't fight effectively when low).
  - Gold threshold for item power spikes (e.g., 800-1200 gold for core items).
  - Before objective spawns (to be at full strength for contests).
  - When enemy jungler has tempo advantage (to reset and avoid being caught out).
- **Base efficiency**: 
  - Base instantly when needed—don't delay by clearing extra camps first.
  - Time your base arrival to coincide with camp respawns so you return to a freshly cleared jungle.
  - Use base time to purchase items that significantly impact clear speed (e.g., Long Sword for early clear, Mythic items for mid-game).
- **Tempo management**: Proper base timers prevent camping downtime—"if you die, enemy knows where you are and you'll be back in base, meaning your camps won't respawn, putting you far behind in gold and XP."

### Common Mistakes
- **Taking extra camps when down tempo**: Clearing additional camps after an objective when you should be basing to contest the next objective or assist lanes.
- **Ignoring camp timers for ganks**: Forgoing camp clears to chase low-percentage ganks, resulting in lost gold/XP and falling behind.
- **Poor pathing causing camp desync**: Clearing camps in inefficient orders that cause them to respawn at staggered times, creating downtime between clears.
- **Not tracking enemy jungler**: Failing to monitor enemy jungler's position and clear speed, leading to unsafe invades or missed counter-jungle opportunities.
- **Basing too early/late**: 
  - Basing with full health and no item needs, wasting map pressure.
  - Delaying base when low on HP or needing items, dying unnecessarily in fights or missing power spikes.
- **Over-reliance on ganking**: Forcing ganks on strong lanes or when enemy jungler has priority, resulting in deaths and lost tempo.
- **Neglecting HP preservation**: Taking unnecessary damage during clears that prevents immediate ganking or invading afterward.
- **Misjudging lane states for ganks**: Attempting ganks on lanes with healthy waves or vision when the enemy jungler is nearby and can counter-gank.

### Quick Reference
- **Clear speed is tempo**: Every second faster in clearing creates potential kill opportunities—prioritize efficient clears over forced ganks.
- **Sync your camps**: Clear in patterns that cause camps to respawn simultaneously to minimize downtime between clears.
- **Always track enemy jungler**: After every camp clear, ask "What can the enemy jungler do right now?" to inform your next decision.
- **Base for power spikes, not habit**: Base only when low HP, needing an item for an objective, or to reset tempo—never base just because you finished a clear.
- **Skip extra camps when behind on tempo**: After objectives/kills, if enemy can move first, base immediately rather than taking additional camps.

##Late Game & Teamfighting

### Baron/Elder Dance Fundamentals
- **Tank Check First**: Before attempting Baron/Elder, verify if your team can tank it. If not, abandon the attempt immediately—enemy team will collapse and kill you.
- **Force Engagement**: If you can tank it, check if you can drop to ~3K HP to force enemy team to come fight you. This creates a 5v5 scenario where you control the engagement.
- **Engagement Options When Enemy Comes**:
  - **Turn and Fight**: If you believe you can win the teamfight (consider ultimates, items, numbers), engage immediately when they commit.
  - **Finish and Zone**: If you're ahead but unsure about winning a fight, finish the objective, zone the enemy jungler, then either retreat or fight after based on the outcome.
- **Disengage When Necessary**: If you cannot force the enemy to come (can't drop to sufficient HP), back off immediately (press B)—forcing a Baron/Elder without threat is suicide.
- **Walking Timings**: 
  - Base to Baron/Elder: 16-17 seconds standard
  - With homeguards: ~13 seconds
  - Without proper boots: 20-22 seconds
  - With dash champions: Can be significantly faster (factor in your mobility)
- **Pre-Objective Preparation**: Always base first to ensure full items/health before attempting Baron/Elder. Never start it while low on resources.

### Objective Control & Vision
- **River Control Wave**: Push the first wave before attempting Baron/Elder to establish river control and force enemy laners to respond.
- **Vision Denial**: 
  - Clear all wards (especially control wards) around the objective before starting it.
  - Place a pink ward on the objective pit—this expires enemy blue trinkets on contact.
  - If you have pink ward and enemy uses blue trinket, their vision will expire in ~8-10 seconds.
- **Hide-and-Seek Tactic**:
  1. Push wave to establish lane priority
  2. Clear all vision around objective
  3. Walk toward objective (do not stand in bush after clearing wards—lingering vision remains)
  4. Bait enemy into face-checking by appearing to commit
  5. Collapse on them when they engage
- **Communication Protocol**: 
  - Ping teammates who move toward objective too early ("Assist me" ping on bush)
  - Use clear, timely pings to coordinate hide-and-seek execution
  - Call out when enemy vision is denied ("Their vision is down")
- **Vision Trapping**: 
  - Hit enemy blue trinket to make them see you
  - Hit Baron/Elder for 2 seconds, then leave immediately to trap over-aggressive enemies
  - Only commit fully when you have vision advantage or enemy is committed elsewhere

### Teamfight Positioning & Engagement
- **Objective as Bait**: Baron/Elder's primary value is forcing enemy engagement—if they don't come to contest, you can take it safely with minimal risk.
- **Steal Attempts**: Enemy 3v5 steal attempts on Baron/Elder almost always fail—your team should collapse immediately and win the ensuing fight.
- **Losing Team Strategy**: When behind, avoid fighting at the objective. Instead, look for teamfights in mid lane where you have better numbers and escape routes.
- **Winning Team Strategy**: When ahead, use vision control to bait enemies into poor engages at the objective, then punish with superior numbers.
- **Post-Fight Objective**: After winning a teamfight near Baron/Elder, you can safely take it—enemy must respawn or concede.
- **Cooldown Awareness**: 
  - Always check enemy ultimate timers before committing to fights
  - If enemy has all ultimates available, they may base instantly and look to fight through mid lane
  - Delay engages if your key ultimates are down unless you have overwhelming advantage
- **Overstay Punishment**: 
  - Collapse on laners who overstay in lane (e.g., pushing too deep without vision)
  - This is especially effective after 14 minutes when tower dive damage increases significantly
- **Herald/Elder Priority**: 
  - If mid tower is up and Herald/Elder is spawning, almost always play for the objective to break mid tower
  - General rule: When mid tower is up, prioritize Herald/Elder if contestable
  - If no Herald/Elder up, play mid lane into the side lane tower that's still up to force enemy to catch wave

### Peel & Protect Strategies
- **Carry Protection**: Use Baron/Elder threat to zone enemy engages—position between enemy engages and your carries.
- **Peel Focus**: Prioritize stopping enemy engages on your carries over chasing fleeing enemies. Your value is in keeping your damage dealers alive.
- **Terrain Utilization**: 
  - Force enemies through narrow paths (river entrances, jungle choke points) when peeling
  - Use terrain to split enemy teams during engages
- **Communication**: 
  - Clearly assign peel responsibilities ("I'll zone their engage, you follow up on their backline")
  - Call out when enemy engages are coming ("Their engage is coming in 3 seconds")
- **Counter-Peel**: If enemy is diving your backline, consider counter-engaging on their frontline instead of purely reacting—this can turn the fight in your favor.

### Common Mistakes
- **Starting Baron/Elder without vision control**: Attempting objectives while enemy has vision control almost always results in steals or lost teamfights.
- **Ignoring lingering vision**: Standing in bushes after clearing wards gets you picked off by enemy vision that hasn't faded yet.
- **Forcing fights without advantage**: Engaging at Baron/Elder when enemy has equal/better ultimates, numbers, or positioning.
- **Overcommitting to steals**: Trying to steal Baron/Elder 3v5 when behind—this consistently loses the fight and the objective.
- **Neglecting wave state**: Not pushing waves before objectives, giving enemy laners free roam to collapse.
- **Poor communication**: Failing to ping teammates who move too early or not calling out vision control status.
- **Basing too late**: Waiting until low HP/items to base before Baron/Elder attempts, reducing your threat level.
- **Misjudging timings**: Not accounting for walking time to objective, leading to late engages or missed opportunities.
- **Fighting in enemy territory**: Attempting teamfights in enemy jungle when behind instead of pulling to your own side of the map.
- **Overlooking tower dive power**: Not taking advantage of increased tower dive damage after 14 minutes to create pressure.

## Mental Framework & Decision-Making

### Core Decision Frameworks

#### The What/How/When Concept
- **What**: Identify what you're playing for (objective, lane pressure, win condition) after every significant game event (kill, death, objective, base)
- **How**: Determine execution method (which lanes to push, when to group, vision control needs)
- **When**: Establish timing (specific wave to contest, rotation timing based on enemy respawns)
- Apply this framework consistently - it's the foundation for all mid-game decision making
- Example: After taking Herald, ask "What are we playing for?" (mid tower), "How" (push mid wave first), "When" (on this specific wave)

#### Tempo Management Framework
Tempo = Who can move first on the map and gain man advantage
- **Jungle down Tempo**: Jungler farming/basing → Play safe, don't overextend lanes
- **Objective down Tempo**: After taking objective (need to clear wave/base) → Enemy can move first
- **Base down Tempo**: You're in base while enemy is on map → Communicate danger to team immediately
- **Death Tempo**: Enemy died and respawning → They may have Tempo advantage
- **Key action**: When down Tempo, ping laners to be careful and play safe
- **Key action**: When up Tempo, look for aggressive plays and roaming opportunities
- **Measurement**: Compare death timers + base time to determine Tempo advantage/disadvantage

#### Win Condition Recognition
- Before every major decision, ask: "What is our win condition?"
- Win conditions include: objective control, side lane pressure, mid lane dominance, split pushing
- Align all pathing and decision-making with your team's current win condition
- If win condition is mid lane pressure: Focus there, don't get distracted by side lanes
- If win condition is side lane: Prioritize that lane, avoid unnecessary mid lane presence
- Never force plays that don't serve your win condition (e.g., forcing mid fights when win condition is side lane pressure)

#### Ping Pong Strategy (Pressure Transfer)
- Use winning lane to create pressure that transfers to other lanes for man advantage
- Requirements:
  1. One winning lane that can push wave and move with jungler
  2. Jungler must clear camps first to be available for rotation
  3. Side laner must play safe until jungler arrives
- Execution: Winning lane pushes → creates pressure → side laner rotates → creates man advantage elsewhere
- Most effective when mid lane is winning (faster wave after 14 minutes enables quicker rotations)
- Critical: Side laner awareness - they must know when team is coming through mid lane

### Adapting to Game State

#### Wave State Analysis (Pre-Gank Checklist)
- **Slow push toward enemy**: Gankable (enemy overextended, vulnerable)
- **Even wave**: Standard gank conditions (check vision and enemy positioning)
- **Slow push toward you**: Dangerous (enemy safe under tower, high risk)
- **Fast push toward you**: Enemy vulnerable to dive (enemy pushing into your territory)
- Always check wave state before ganking - it determines enemy positioning and kill potential
- Example: If wave is slow pushing toward enemy, they're likely overextended and gankable

#### Jungler Tracking System
- Track enemy jungler's likely start (wolves/blue vs red/gromp based on invade potential)
- Monitor camp timers: Note when enemy jungler finishes clears to predict gank timing
- Use wave states to anticipate enemy jungler's path (pushing lanes = likely gank direction)
- Know enemy jungler identity: Early game ganker (look for early invades) vs farmer (focus on tracking later)
- Vision confirmation: Place wards at common jungle entrances to verify location
- Critical action: Ping laners when enemy jungler can be on their lane based on your tracking

#### Objective-Based Decision Protocol
- **Before taking objective**: Always ask "What are we playing for after this?"
- **After Baron**: Identify safest tower to take (usually side tower - less collapse risk than mid)
- **After Dragon**: Decide immediately: push lane, take turret, or group for next objective
- **Vision prerequisite**: Establish deep vision control before committing to objectives
- **Exit plan**: Know exactly where to rotate if enemy collapses on your position
- **Baron-specific**: Push mid lane wave first for river control before attempting Baron

#### Base Timers and Itemization Protocol
- Base when: Low HP (<40%), need power spike item, camps are far away (>15s walk), objective about to spawn (<30s)
- **Critical rule**: When basing, immediately think "What am I playing for next?" before leaving base
- **Avoid**: Taking extra camps when you need to base for upcoming objective (loses Tempo)
- **Optimal base timing**: Base on fresh waves (not mid-wave) to maximize pressure upon return
- **Tempo gain**: Properly timed base can give you Tempo advantage by returning before enemy can act

### Mental Approach and Self-Improvement

#### Structured Review Process (The 3 Deaths Review)
1. **Immediately after game**: Review your first three deaths
2. **For each death, analyze three layers**:
   - **Micro**: Spell usage (correct cooldowns?), dodging enemy abilities, positioning
   - **Macro**: Gold spending efficiency, number advantage played with, playing with strongest member
   - **Decision-making**: What were you thinking in-game? Did you anticipate threats? Did you stick to game plan?
3. **Identify 1-2 concrete improvements** per game (not vague "play better")
4. **Focus execution**: Work on those specific improvements in next 5-10 games before adding new ones

#### Champion Identity and Playstyle Adaptation
- **Early game gankers** (Lee Sin, Elise, level 2-3 strong):
  - Look for early opportunities but DON'T force ganks without proper conditions
  - Falling behind on failed ganks is catastrophic - only take high-percentage plays
- **Farming champions** (Lilia, Kindred, level 6 power spikes):
  - Prioritize reaching power spike before forcing aggressive plays
  - Know your weak windows (pre-level 6) and play accordingly
  - Don't over-gank - falling behind defeats your late-game strength
- **Universal rule**: Know your damage output limits for successful ganks
  - Test in practice tool: "Can I kill this laner with my combo + their laner's damage?"
  - If no, don't force the gank - wait for better opportunity or more items

#### Tilt Management and Decision Discipline
- **Stick to your game plan** unless game state changes significantly (objective taken, major kill)
- **Don't chase low-percentage plays** just because you're ahead - preserves leads
- **Recognize when to play safe vs aggressive** based on:
  - Current Tempo (up/down)
  - Win condition alignment
  - Enemy vision and potential counter-play
- **Critical communication**: Ping laners your intentions:
  - "Basing for item X" (prevents overextension)
  - "Rotating to your lane" (enables follow-up)
  - "Enemy jungler here" (enables safe play)
- **Ego check**: Sometimes letting a teammate die is correct if it preserves your win condition
  - Example: If bot lane is lost but your win condition is mid lane pressure, don't throw mid trying to save bot

### Common Mistakes

1. **Ignoring Tempo**: Overextending when down Tempo (jungle/objective/base down), leading to unnecessary deaths and lost objectives
2. **Static win condition**: Continuing to play for outdated objectives after game state changes (e.g., still pushing mid after losing turret when win condition shifted to side lane)
3. **Poor base timing**: Taking "just one more camp" when you need to base for power spike item or upcoming objective (loses Tempo and map pressure)
4. **Force-ganking**: Ganking without proper wave state, vision confirmation, or kill potential (results in death or wasted time)
5. **Jungler blind spots**: Not tracking enemy jungler through wave states, camp timers, and vision (results in surprise invades/counter-ganks)
6. **Baron misuse**: Taking Baron without clear follow-up plan, vision control, or understanding of what to do after (leads to throws)
7. **Over-ganking with farmers**: Falling behind by forcing early ganks with champions that need to scale (Lilia, Kindred)
8. **Communication failure**: Not pinging laners about your intentions (basing, rotating, needing help) leading to misplays
9. **Kill obsession**: Prioritizing kills over objectives, turret plates, and map pressure (loses long-term advantage)
10. **Reactive playing**: Not having a pre-game plan and reacting to events instead of proactively creating advantages

### Quick Reference

1. **Apply What/How/When religiously** after every kill, death, objective, or base - this is your decision-making anchor
2. **When down Tempo**: Immediately play safe, ping laners to be careful, and avoid overextending - your job is to survive until Tempo returns
3. **Post-objective protocol**: Before taking any objective, know exactly what you're playing for after it (tower, lane pressure, next objective)
4. **Ping pong execution**: Only initiate when you have a winning lane that can push - side laner MUST play safe until you arrive for the pressure transfer to work
5. **Base on fresh waves**: Always base when a wave is just clearing (not mid-wave) to maximize pressure and Tempo advantage upon return
6. **Structured self-review**: After every game, review first 3 deaths across micro (mechanics), macro (decision-making), and mental (what you were thinking) - identify 1-2 specific improvements
7. **Win condition fidelity**: Never deviate from your team's win condition without significant game state change - forcing off-condition plays loses games
8. **Jungler tracking trifecta**: Monitor wave states (predict path), camp timers (predict timing), and vision (confirm location) - ping laners when threat exists
9. **Gank discipline**: Only gank when wave state favors you, vision shows enemy position, and you have confirmed kill potential (damage + CC)
10. **Clear communication**: Use pings and short chat to convey your intentions (basing, rotating, needing help) - prevents misplays and enables follow-up

  - Presence of crowd control increases volatility (makes it easier to kill)
  - Example: Top lane ranged vs melee with CC on melee side = volatile
- Determine your team's swing condition (what your team wants to do)
  - Ask: Do we win 3v2 doing 3v3? Do we win 4v4 on objectives?

### Champion Matchup Knowledge
- Lane volatility assessment:

  - Stable lanes: Safe farming lanes where champions can play passive
  - Example: Bot lane with Pike vs Draven/Nami = volatile (will be a "show")
- Matchup-specific considerations:
  - Range vs range matchups: Path towards the lane that has push (whoever is pushing properly has advantage)

  - If both top players have teleport: Different considerations (less volatile for invades)
  - Assassins: Don't want to team fight unless good position or team comp strong

    - If lose team fight, cross map
  - Tanks: Better in team fights (front to back)

- Champion identity and gank potential:
  - Farmers (need level 6): Lower chance of successful ganks pre-6, but can gank if opportunity arises
    - Examples: Kindred, Lillia (need level 6 ideally, but can gank with low success rate pre-6)
    - Philosophy: Don't look for gank unless free kill opportunity arises
  - Street kill (level 2/3 gankers): Higher early gank potential
    - Examples: Most junglers (Lee Sin, Elise, etc.)
    - Key: Don't have to gank if no opportunity; take camps and look for gank later
  - Important: Every jungler should look for gank only if they think it's a free kill
    - Over-ganking when behind loses the game (dying means enemy knows your position)
