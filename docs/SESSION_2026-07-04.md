## 1. Decisions & analysis from the conversation (compressed)

- **Strategy order changed:** validate demand BEFORE building M1–M4. Demo path =
  manual CLI-powered Reddit giveaway + landing page + waitlist. See
  [PROMO_DRAFTS.md](PROMO_DRAFTS.md) (includes the "sell the WHAT, never the HOW"
  disclosure principle).
- **Monetization verified:** subscriptions, donations, crowdfunding all allowed by
  Riot policy; ads allowed on free tier but NOT in-game/overlay (2025 ban), and a
  Discord bot has no ad surface anyway. **Registration with Riot is required even
  for a free product.** Registration is fast; only the Production key is slow.
- **Competitive position:** iTero+ ($2.99, drafts/overlay) and Mobalytics (~$6–10,
  stat dashboards) don't do narrative timestamped per-game reviews. We compete
  with human VOD reviews at 1/50th the price, not with dashboards.
- **Role expansion:** frame publicly as "macro coach, jungle first". Jungle is the
  role best served by 60s-granularity timeline data; laner skill (waves, trading)
  is invisible to the API. Expand only after jungle validates; keep service code
  role-agnostic in naming.
- **IP/partnership:** ideas/methods aren't copyrightable; risk is reputational
  (community recognizing uncredited methodology), not legal. Contact coaches
  before launch: ask blessing+attribution first, offer affiliate commission
  (20–30% via Paddle coupon) second. Multiple coaches also fixes the
  one-coach-deep moat.
- **Killer feature candidate:** multi-game pattern review ("why do I keep
  losing") — built this session as `scripts/account_recap.py`; natural paid-tier
  anchor; neither competitor does it as narrative.
